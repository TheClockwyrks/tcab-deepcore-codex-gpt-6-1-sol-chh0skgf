# Deepcore

The rescue signal is dead. Your dropship is scrap. Beneath your boots lies a planet full of minerals—and the parts of your last chance to leave. Take a drill and a jetpack into the dark, and build your own way home.

Cut a shaft through weathered topsoil and follow mineral veins into colder, harder rock. A rich cargo buys better equipment, but every kilogram slows the climb. Watch your fuel, leave yourself a route back, and know when to turn around. Gravity will take you deeper for free. Getting home is your problem.

The surface camp is your lifeline: sell ore, buy fuel and repairs, expand your cargo bay, strengthen your hull, and assemble the escape rocket one component at a time. A scanner can guide you toward the rare crystals buried far below. Supplies offer another way out when a promising dig becomes a bad bet.

Past the gray rockbed, gas breathes through the stone and lava lights the coreshell. At the bottom waits a prize that will not stay stable for long. Bring the Core Sample back before its ninety seconds run out, install the ignition, and leave this world behind.

Choose a short, standard, or marathon descent. Take the safety of a saved expedition in Standard, or risk everything in Hardcore. There are no enemies down here. The mine is enough.
